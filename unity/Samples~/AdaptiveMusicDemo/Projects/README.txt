Adaptive Music Demo projects

unity-demo-1.melo and unity-demo-2.melo use the Samples, Soundfonts, and
Impulses folders beside them. Keep these folders together.

Import the Adaptive Music Demo from the package's Samples tab and open
Scenes/SwitchSongs.unity. The scene demonstrates immediate switching,
bar-aligned switching, fades, crossfades, volume, and tempo.

See Licenses/SAMPLE-CONTENT-NOTICES.txt in the package for the demo content terms.
