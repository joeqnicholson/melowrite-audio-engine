# Adaptive Music Demo

Import it from the Melowrite package's Samples tab and open `Scenes/SwitchSongs.unity`. `Scripts/Music.cs` drives a `MeloSource` between two songs.

The buttons switch songs right away, on the next bar, with a crossfade, or with a fade out. The sliders are master volume and tempo. Both `.melo` files and their samples, soundfont and impulses are in `Projects/`.

Needs Unity 2021.3 or newer on Windows x64. Install steps are in the package's `README.md`.
