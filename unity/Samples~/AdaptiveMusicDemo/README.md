# Adaptive Music Demo

Open `Scenes/SwitchSongs.unity` after importing this sample from the Melowrite Audio Engine package. The scene uses `Scripts/Music.cs` and a `MeloSource` to play two Melowrite projects.

Use the buttons to switch songs immediately, switch at a bar boundary, crossfade, or fade out. The sliders control master volume and tempo. The two `.melo` files and their samples, SoundFont, and impulses are in `Projects/`.

Unity 2021.3 or newer and Windows x64 are supported. See the package's `README.md` for installation instructions.
