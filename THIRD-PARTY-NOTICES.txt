SNES SPC700 echo  -  https://github.com/nyanpasu64-backup/snes-echo
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "SNES Echo" effect is an original C# implementation of the SNES SPC700 DSP echo
(echo buffer + 8-tap FIR + feedback), written from the documented hardware
behavior. nyanpasu64's snes-echo is acknowledged as a reference for the design.
